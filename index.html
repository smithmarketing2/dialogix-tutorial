Getting Started in 5 Steps

From signup to your first AI call in under 10 minutes

1

Create Your Account

Sign up at dialogix.ai with your business email. No credit card required for the trial.

# Visit the signup page https://dialogix.ai/signup # Or contact our team for a demo contact team@dialogix.ai
2

Configure Your Agent

Set up your AI voice agent with your business details, greeting script, and conversation flow.

# Agent Configuration Business Name: Smith Roofing Greeting: "Thanks for calling Smith Roofing..." Hours: Mon-Fri 8AM-6PM Services: Roof repair, replacement, inspection
3

Connect Your Phone Number

Port your existing number or get a new one. Dialogix provides local and toll-free numbers.

# Phone Number Setup Option 1: Port existing number Option 2: Get new local number Option 3: Use toll-free number # Forward calls to Dialogix Set call forwarding: YOUR_NUMBER → DIALOGIX_NUMBER
4

Build Conversation Flows

Design how your agent handles different call types using our visual flow builder.

# Sample Conversation Flow IF caller wants quote → Ask: property type, issue description Collect: name, phone, email Action: send lead to CRM IF caller wants to schedule → Check calendar availability Book appointment Send confirmation SMS
5

Go Live & Monitor

Activate your agent and watch the dashboard for real-time call data and insights.
